Events & fundraising

Come out to the park.

Falkner Heritage Park events are fundraisers. They are one of the ways this organization raises the money to build the Community Center and Museum Project — every car show, every festival, every plate served goes into the work.

Every year

The gatherings that come around again

Dates are set year to year and announced first in the Facebook group. Call 662-587-4067 if you would like to be told directly.

Founders Day Fall Celebration

The park’s largest gathering of the year and the anchor of the Falkner Heritage calendar. A car show, food vendors, crafts, yard sales, raffles, and educational activities for children — a full day in Old Town Falkner.

Date: announced each year in the Facebook group.

The Quilt Expo

Quilts brought in by families from across Tippah County and hung in the Cooper Hill School House — the sort of exhibit that old room was practically built for. Bring a quilt, or come and look.

Date: announced each year in the Facebook group.

Museum tours and group visits

Outside of scheduled events, the Museum and the Cooper Hill School House are opened for group tours and by appointment. Schools, church groups, family reunions and history clubs are all welcome — call 662-587-4067 to arrange a time.

Where the money goes

Every event is a fundraiser.

Falkner Heritage is a non-profit organization and receives no revenue from the Town. Funding for the Falkner Heritage Park projects has come entirely from proceeds from supporters and volunteers, local businesses and community efforts, banks, schools and churches, project sales and special events — with continued generous donations making the work possible.

Events are one of those sources. Phase 1 of the Community Center — the foundation and slab — was paid for this way and is complete. Phase 2, framing and roofing, is next.
